## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the river's stage record of that morning: a chart-recorder sheet where one pen traces the flood front against the clock. It refuses the dark satellite story map with stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pale green ruled chart paper (1/5/10 mm) in the frame and on chart plates, never behind body text. Graphite ink. Pen 1, carmine, is the one committed spot colour: the front, our footprint, the cursor. Pen 2, recorder blue: observations and Copernicus. Optical and radar are hatches at 45° and −45°, and "both" is the cross-hatch. Condensed grotesk caps for literal name plates and station headers, tabular figures for readings, a serif reading face for the column. The map is a hillshade inked in the paper's own greens.

STORY: The reader learns that the front reached Syabrubesi at 08:49, 26 minutes before the 09:15 SMS. They see where the ground changed, how it was checked against Copernicus, and what lay inside the change, then scrub time to watch it happen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Recorder header strip across the top (RECORD · RIVER · DATE · START 08:37 NPT). Left: title, standfirst, contents as a list of stations. Right, about 60 % of the width: the master trace (distance down the river against minutes) on ruled paper, the pen drawing on load, the SMS line struck at 09:15. A margin numeral "26" in carmine, about 12× body size. The pen scrubber runs along the bottom of the trace.

FORM: Own grounded list, candidate 5 of 7 (hydrological stage record). Seed key 3bbb6e55. Raises: literal name plates (streetwear quote grammar); one committed spot colour (Art Paul program); margin numerals at ground scale (Botteghe quarterly); a scrubber that snaps to whole minutes and re-flags where you stopped (cutting bench); optical and radar as crossing hatches (darkroom exposure record).
